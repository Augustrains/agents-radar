Rather than building individual agents, developers are now building systems that *generate* agents. **2. Agentic video production emerges.** `OpenMontage` (+3,719 stars in one day) is not just a video tool; it's an **agentic production system** with 52 tools and 500+ agent skills. The second wave of AI agents isn't about better prompts — it's about agents that **remember, learn, and adapt across sessions**.
