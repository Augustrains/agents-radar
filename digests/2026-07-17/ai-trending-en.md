## 3. Trend Signal Analysis

**The dominant signal today is the rise of "AI Skills" as a new software distribution model.** Both today's trending list and the broader topic search reveal a paradigm shift: developers are no longer just building standalone AI applications or agents — they are building composable, sharable **skills** that plug into existing AI coding assistants. Projects like **hallmark**, **skills**, **Graphify**, and **ui-skills** represent a new category of AI middleware that modifies model behavior and capabilities on-the-fly. The explosive growth of **hallmark** (+3,372 stars in a single day) and **skills** (+2,060) suggests the community is hungry for quality-control layers that improve AI-generated code and design output.

**The "Skills" ecosystem is coalescing around Claude Code as the primary target**, with most projects explicitly supporting Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and Gemini CLI. This mirrors the plugin ecosystem dynamics of early VS Code or jQuery, but for AI agents. The **Copilot SDK** release from GitHub further validates this direction — platform vendors are now enabling third-party integration at the infrastructure level.

**A second strong signal is Graphify's explosive growth (+1,107 today)**, representing convergence between RAG and agent tooling. By turning entire codebases into queryable knowledge graphs accessible from coding assistants, it solves the "context window" problem for large projects — a pain point many developers face.

**DeepTutor (+656 today)** signals renewed interest in AI-powered education, specifically **lifelong personalized tutoring** — a more ambitious framing than simple Q&A chatbots.

**Notably absent** from today's trending: pure model releases, fine-tuning tools, and traditional ML frameworks (PyTorch, TensorFlow are in topic search but not trending). The community's attention has shifted from "how to train models" to "how to make existing models more useful and controllable."
